Frequently asked questions

Do I need internet or Wi‑Fi to play?
No. Cachito connects nearby phones directly over Bluetooth — no internet, no account, no server involved at any point.
A friend can’t see my game in their list — what do I check?
Make sure Bluetooth is turned on for both phones, that they’re within a few meters of each other, and that the host has already tapped “Host a Game” before the other player opens “Join a Game.”
How many players can join one game?
2 to 10 players at the same table.
Is there a Spanish version?
Yes — tap the language button on the home screen to switch between English and Spanish at any time.
What happens if a player disconnects mid‑game?
The game continues without them. A player who leaves can’t rejoin the same game, but is welcome back once a new game starts.

Preguntas frecuentes

¿Necesito internet o Wi‑Fi para jugar?
No. Cachito conecta los teléfonos cercanos directamente por Bluetooth — sin internet, sin cuenta, sin ningún servidor de por medio.
Un amigo no ve mi partida en su lista — ¿qué reviso?
Asegúrate de que el Bluetooth esté activado en ambos teléfonos, que estén a pocos metros de distancia, y que el anfitrión ya haya tocado “Empieza un Juego” antes de que el otro jugador abra “Entra al Juego.”
¿Cuántos jugadores pueden unirse a una partida?
De 2 a 10 jugadores en la misma mesa.
