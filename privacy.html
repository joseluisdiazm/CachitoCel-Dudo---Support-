Cachito — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Short version: Cachito does not collect, store, or transmit any of your personal data, to us or to anyone else.

How the game works

Cachito connects players’ phones directly to each other over Bluetooth. There is no account to create, no login, no server, and no internet connection involved in playing a game. All game data (dice rolls, calls, player names typed into the app) stays on the phones at the table and is never sent anywhere else.

What we access, and why

  • Bluetooth — used only to discover and connect to nearby phones so you can host or join a game. It is never used for anything else, and never leaves your device to reach us.
  • Motion sensors (accelerometer) — used only to detect a “shake to roll” gesture during gameplay. Nothing is recorded or transmitted.

What we don’t do

  • No account creation, no sign‑in.
  • No analytics or tracking of any kind.
  • No advertising or ad networks.
  • No data shared with third parties, because no player data ever reaches us in the first place.

Children’s privacy

Cachito does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children, since it collects none at all.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Cachito ever adds a feature that uses the internet or collects any data, this page will be updated first, before that feature ships.
